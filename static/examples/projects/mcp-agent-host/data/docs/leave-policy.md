# Leave policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees get 25 days of annual leave a year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year and expire on 31 March.

## Requesting leave
Request leave in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days. Your manager approves or declines within five working days.

## Sick leave
Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. From the fourth consecutive day you need a doctor's note.

## Parental leave
Primary carers get 20 weeks at full pay; secondary carers get 6 weeks. Leave can start up to two weeks before the due date.
