# Expenses policy

## Claim deadline
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims older than 90 days are rejected unless your manager approves an exception in writing.

## Receipts
Every claim over 25 EUR needs an itemised receipt. Photos from the mobile app are accepted. Card slips without item lines are not.

## Travel
Book economy class for flights under six hours. Hotels are capped at 180 EUR a night in capital cities and 130 EUR elsewhere. Taxis are allowed after 21:00 or when carrying equipment.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance on business travel is 55 EUR. Alcohol is never reimbursed.
