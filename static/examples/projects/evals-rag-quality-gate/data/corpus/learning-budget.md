# Learning and Development

Each employee has a personal learning budget of 1,200 GBP per calendar year for courses, conferences, books and certifications. The budget does not roll over into the next year.

Spending from the learning budget needs manager approval in advance through Harbour. Books under 40 GBP can be bought without prior approval and claimed through Ledgerly.

You also get 5 learning days per year, which are paid working days reserved for study or attending training.

If the company pays more than 3,000 GBP for a single qualification and you resign within 12 months of completing it, you repay a share of the cost on a sliding scale.

Internal mentoring is available to everyone. Mentoring pairs are matched each quarter by People Operations.
