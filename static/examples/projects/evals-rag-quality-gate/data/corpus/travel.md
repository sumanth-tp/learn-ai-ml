# Business Travel Policy

All business travel is booked through the travel desk portal, Wayfarer, so that the company knows where employees are in an emergency.

Train travel is standard class. First class is allowed only for journeys longer than 3 hours. Flights under 6 hours are economy; flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy.

Hotel rates are capped at 180 GBP per night in London and 130 GBP per night elsewhere in the United Kingdom. International caps are listed in Wayfarer for each city.

While travelling you may claim a meal allowance of up to 45 GBP per day for food, using the expenses process in Ledgerly.

Travel to a destination rated high-risk by the security team requires written approval from a director and a security briefing at least 5 working days before departure.

Mileage for business journeys in a private car is reimbursed at 45 pence per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year.
