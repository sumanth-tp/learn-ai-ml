# Employee Benefits

Fernhill contributes 6 percent of base salary to the company pension scheme when you contribute at least 4 percent. Enrolment is automatic after your first month.

Private medical insurance covers you from your first day. You may add a partner or children to the policy; the company pays for the employee and 50 percent of the cost for dependants.

Every employee has access to the Employee Assistance Programme, a confidential counselling service available 24 hours a day by phone.

The cycle-to-work scheme lets you buy a bicycle up to 2,000 GBP through salary sacrifice, repaid over 12 months.

Life assurance pays four times your base salary. It applies from your first day of employment and needs no enrolment.

Benefits are reviewed once a year in October, and changes take effect on 1 January.
