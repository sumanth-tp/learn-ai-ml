# Code of Conduct

Fernhill expects everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated in any setting, including online chat and company events.

You may accept gifts or hospitality from suppliers worth up to 50 GBP. Anything above 50 GBP must be declared in the gifts register in Harbour within 5 working days, and gifts above 150 GBP must be politely declined.

Conflicts of interest, such as a family member working for a supplier, must be declared to your manager and to Legal as soon as you become aware of them.

Concerns about misconduct can be raised with your manager, with People Operations, or anonymously through the Speak Up line, which is run by an independent provider. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.

Breaches of this code may lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.
