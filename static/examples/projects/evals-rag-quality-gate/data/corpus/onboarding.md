# Onboarding Guide

Your first day starts at 09:30 at reception, where your onboarding buddy meets you. Your buddy is a colleague from a neighbouring team who helps you for your first 3 months.

In the first week you complete the security awareness training, set up Keystone and multi-factor authentication, and meet your manager to agree 30-60-90 day goals.

The probation period is 6 months. Your manager holds probation reviews at 3 months and at 6 months. Probation can be extended once, by up to 3 months.

During probation the notice period on either side is 1 week. After probation it becomes 1 month, or 3 months for managers and above.

New joiners attend a company induction day, held on the first Tuesday of every month, where each department introduces its work.
