# Working Hours and Overtime

The standard working week is 37.5 hours, typically Monday to Friday, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break each day.

Salaried employees do not receive paid overtime. Instead, time off in lieu can be agreed with your manager for work outside normal hours, such as a weekend release. Time off in lieu must be taken within 3 months.

Employees on the on-call rota receive an allowance of 250 GBP per week of on-call cover, plus time off in lieu for any incident work outside working hours longer than 2 hours.

Nobody should work more than 48 hours per week averaged over 17 weeks. If you regularly exceed this, raise it with your manager.

Requests for flexible working, such as compressed hours or a four-day week, can be made from your first day and are answered within 8 weeks.
