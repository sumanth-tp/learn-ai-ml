# Parental Leave

Birth parents with at least 26 weeks of service receive 26 weeks of maternity leave at full pay, followed by up to 13 weeks at statutory pay.

Partners and second parents receive 8 weeks of paternity or partner leave at full pay, which can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

Adoptive parents receive the same entitlements as birth parents, with the primary adopter entitled to 26 weeks at full pay.

Tell People Operations about planned parental leave at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth, or within 7 days of being matched for adoption.

On return, parents may request a phased return of up to 4 weeks at reduced hours on full pay. Up to 10 keeping-in-touch days can be worked during leave without ending it.
