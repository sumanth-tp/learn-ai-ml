# Performance and Pay Reviews

Performance is reviewed twice a year: a mid-year check-in in June and a full review in December. Both are recorded in Harbour.

The full review uses four ratings: Exceeding, Strong, Developing and Below Expectations. Ratings are calibrated across each department before they are shared with employees.

Pay reviews happen once a year, and salary changes take effect on 1 April. Employees rated Below Expectations are not eligible for a pay increase in that cycle.

If your rating is Below Expectations, your manager agrees a performance improvement plan with you lasting 8 to 12 weeks, with fortnightly check-ins.

You may appeal a rating within 10 working days of receiving it by writing to your skip-level manager. The appeal outcome is final.

Promotions are considered at the December review. A promotion case needs evidence against the career framework and sign-off by the department head.
