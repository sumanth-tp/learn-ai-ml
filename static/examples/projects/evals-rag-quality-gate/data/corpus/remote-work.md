# Hybrid and Remote Working

Fernhill operates a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the office on at least 2 days per week, and every team agrees one shared anchor day when everyone attends.

You may work fully remotely for up to 4 weeks per calendar year from a country other than your employment country, subject to manager approval and a tax check by People Operations. Requests must be submitted in Harbour at least 15 working days before travel. Working remotely from a country under a sanctions list is never permitted.

Remote workers receive a one-off home office allowance of 400 GBP for furniture such as a desk or chair, claimed through the expenses process within 3 months of joining.

When working remotely you must use the company VPN for any access to internal systems, and you must not work from public networks without the VPN enabled.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local office time zone. Outside core hours you may arrange your working day flexibly.
