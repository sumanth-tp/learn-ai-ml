# IT Equipment and Support

New employees receive a laptop on their first day, either a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1, chosen by the hiring manager. Engineers may request a second external monitor.

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. If a laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the IT service desk within 24 hours so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

The IT service desk is reached through the Beacon ticketing portal. Priority 1 incidents, such as being unable to work at all, are answered within 1 hour during office hours. Other tickets are answered within 1 working day.

You must not install software from outside the approved catalogue in Beacon without a security review.

Personal devices may be used for email and chat only if they are enrolled in mobile device management. Personal devices must never store customer data.

When you leave the company, all equipment must be returned within 5 working days of your last day.
