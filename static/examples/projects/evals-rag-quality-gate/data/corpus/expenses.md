# Expenses Policy

Business expenses are claimed in the Ledgerly expenses app within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted later than 60 days after purchase are rejected.

Every claim needs an itemised receipt. For purchases under 25 GBP without a receipt, you may submit a signed missing-receipt declaration instead, but no more than three times per year.

Claims up to 500 GBP are approved by your line manager. Claims above 500 GBP also need approval from the budget holder in Finance.

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll run, provided they are approved by the 20th of the month.

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client entertainment events approved in advance by a director. Client entertainment is capped at 75 GBP per head including drinks.

Company credit cards are available to employees who travel more than six times per year. Card statements must be reconciled in Ledgerly by the 5th working day of each month.
