# Sickness Absence Policy

If you are too unwell to work, tell your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A message in the team channel is not enough; contact your manager directly by phone or direct message.

You can self-certify sickness absence for up to 7 calendar days. For absences longer than 7 calendar days you must provide a fit note from a doctor, uploaded to Harbour.

Fernhill pays full salary for up to 20 working days of sickness absence in any rolling 12-month period. After that, company sick pay drops to half salary for a further 20 working days, and then to statutory sick pay only.

When you return after an absence of more than 5 working days, your manager holds a short return-to-work conversation to agree any adjustments, such as a phased return.

Medical appointments that cannot be scheduled outside working hours do not count as sickness absence, but you should give your manager as much notice as possible.
