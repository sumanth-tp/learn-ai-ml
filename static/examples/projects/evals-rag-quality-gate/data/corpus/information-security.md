# Information Security Standards

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and stored in the company password manager, Keystone. Reusing a work password on any other site is forbidden.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every company account. Hardware security keys are required for administrators and for anyone with production access.

Report a suspected phishing email with the Report button in the mail client. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, also call the security hotline immediately; do not wait for office hours.

Customer data is classified as Confidential. Confidential data must never be pasted into public AI tools, personal email or unapproved file-sharing services. The approved AI assistant for internal use is the Fernhill Copilot.

All employees complete security awareness training within 14 days of joining and then once every 12 months.

Screens must be locked whenever you leave your device unattended, even in the office.
