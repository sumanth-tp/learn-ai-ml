title: LFP battery procurement review 2025
published: 2025-09-18
---
Our 2025 LFP battery procurement closed at 92 USD per kWh for containerised grid-storage cells, down from 128 USD per kWh in 2023. LFP battery pack prices fell because of cell manufacturing overcapacity.

LFP batteries delivered 6,000 cycles to 80 percent capacity in our existing grid-storage fleet, with round-trip efficiency of 88 to 90 percent at the site level. LFP cost per kWh remains the benchmark any sodium-ion battery offer must beat.

Procurement risk for LFP batteries is concentrated in lithium carbonate price volatility and export controls on battery-grade graphite.
