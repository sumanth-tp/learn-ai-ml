title: Heat pump retrofit programme lessons learned
published: 2025-06-30
---
Our 2024 heat pump retrofit programme installed 1,200 air-source heat pumps in cold-climate homes. Average seasonal coefficient of performance was 2.6, falling to 1.7 on days below minus 15 degrees Celsius.

The main heat pump retrofit risk was undersized radiators: 30 percent of cold-climate homes needed radiator upgrades to reach comfort targets. Installer shortages added an average of nine weeks to each heat pump project.

Heat pump running costs were 18 percent lower than the gas boilers they replaced, but only where the electricity-to-gas price ratio stayed below 3.5.
