title: Sodium-ion battery pilot memo (Grid Storage team)
published: 2025-11-04
---
Sodium-ion battery cells reached commercial grid-storage deployment in 2024, with the first 100 MWh sodium-ion battery projects connected in China. Our grid-storage team rates sodium-ion technology maturity at roughly five years behind lithium iron phosphate (LFP).

Sodium-ion batteries avoid lithium, cobalt and nickel, so sodium-ion supply chain risk is lower than for LFP batteries. Sodium-ion cells use hard carbon anodes, and hard carbon supply is currently concentrated in a small number of producers, which is a supply risk of its own.

Sodium-ion battery energy density is 140 to 160 Wh/kg at cell level, compared with 160 to 190 Wh/kg for LFP cells. For stationary grid storage, lower energy density matters less than for vehicles because site footprint is rarely the binding constraint.
