title: Heat pump grid impact study (Networks)
published: 2025-12-08
---
Electrifying heating with heat pumps raises winter peak demand. Our network study found that every 1,000 cold-climate heat pumps add 1.5 to 2.5 MW of peak load on the coldest evenings.

Heat pump risk for distribution networks is concentrated in older feeders; 12 percent of our feeders would need reinforcement at 40 percent heat pump adoption. Smart thermostats that pre-heat homes cut the heat pump peak by about 20 percent.
