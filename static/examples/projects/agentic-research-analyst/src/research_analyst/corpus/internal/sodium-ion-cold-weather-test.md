title: Sodium-ion cold-weather bench test
published: 2026-02-20
---
In our bench test, sodium-ion battery cells retained 88 percent of rated capacity at minus 20 degrees Celsius, while LFP cells retained 60 percent. Sodium-ion battery performance in cold climates reduces the heating load for outdoor grid-storage enclosures.

Measured sodium-ion battery round-trip efficiency was 86 percent at the cell level. The tested sodium-ion cells reached 3,000 cycles before the test ended, with 91 percent capacity remaining, so cycle life beyond 4,000 cycles is plausible but not yet proven.
